01 / The occasion

People buy to be a good friend.

After a breakup, a friend wants to do something more memorable than send another “you deserve better” text.

WANKER makes that gesture easy to give, photograph, share, and remember. Petty revenge is a side wink. Friendship is the reason to buy.

The product is pre-launch. The gift occasion and positioning are the thesis, not a claim of proven sales.

02 / Cultural signal

Friendship gets its own moment.

Showing up for your friends is having a cover moment.

Vogue Germany devoted its June 2026 issue to friendship, community, and real connection. WANKER gives a friend a tangible way to show up with humor.

03 / Novelty with utility

The gimmick opens the door.

J&D’s Baconlube began as an April Fools’ joke. People asked for it, so they made it real.

The lesson isn't “make bacon-flavored lube.” It’s that an absurd premise can earn attention and become a purchase. WANKER is not bacon-flavored: it is a real water-based lubricant with a joke that travels.

04 / How it sells

From “this is so you”
to checkout.

Breakup gifts and care packages already have shoppers on marketplaces. WANKER’s plan pairs that demand with shareable, founder-led content.

1. Discovery

2. Capture

The gift site collects interested buyers on a pre-launch email waitlist.

3. Purchase & spread

A tube or gift box can become the gift, the unboxing, and the next friend-to-friend referral.
